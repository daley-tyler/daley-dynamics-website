01 Problems worth solving

Most business problems don’t start as technology problems.

They start with a process that takes too long, information nobody can find, data nobody can use, software that no longer fits, or internal systems that have grown without a clear plan.

01

Repetitive manual work

Tasks that happen every day or every week shouldn’t require someone to manually rebuild the same result each time.

02

Information is hard to find

Important knowledge can end up spread across folders, documents, spreadsheets and different people. A searchable system can bring it together and make it useful.

04 How projects work

Understand the business first. Build the technology second.

Projects can range from fixing or automating one troublesome process to designing a larger internal system from the ground up.

  1. 01

    Understand

    The work starts with the problem, current process, constraints and what a successful result would actually look like.

  2. 02

    Design

    The simplest system that solves the problem properly is defined.

  3. 03

    Build

    The system is built around the way the business actually operates.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Real data, workflows, edge cases, access rules and expected behaviour are used to test the result.

  5. 05

    Deploy & Improve

    The result is made usable, documented where it matters and left with room for sensible future growth.
